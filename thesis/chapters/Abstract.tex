\begin{abstract}
This thesis is about the formalization of Hardy spaces and the Nevanlinna
class, with the ultimate goal of formalizing the canonical factorization
theorem for Nevanlinna functions, using the proof assistant Lean
together with its mathematical library Mathlib. We first lay the
foundations of Hardy spaces and Nevanlinna functions, for which we
formalize the notions including subharmonic functions, Poisson integrals,
nontangential limits and the theory surrounding them along the way. Then we
focus on the formalization of the canonical factorization theorem of
Nevanlinna functions, for which we formalize Blaschke products, outer functions
and singular functions, and then state and prove the theorem in full.

In this thesis, we will only present selected parts of the code of the formalization.
For the full formalization, one can refer to
\url{https://github.com/SHW715/HardySpace_formalization} together with its blueprint
\url{https://shw715.github.io/HardySpace_formalization/} (the blueprint webpage is mainly
maintained by AI agents, e.g.\ Codex and Claude Code).
\end{abstract}
